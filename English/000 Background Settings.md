# *Reburn the Galaxy* — World Setting

## Preface

*Reburn the Galaxy* is a long-form fanfiction, first published during an early version of the game *Phoenix II*, before any official story mode had been implemented. As a result, very little canonical setting information existed at the time. All worldbuilding in this work was created by the author, and predates official releases. Due to this, there may be significant differences from the game’s later canon. Readers’ understanding is greatly appreciated.

---

## Historical Background

**Year 1 of the Alliance**, forces from the **Void World** launched their first invasion of the galaxy. The weapons used by the galaxy’s civilizations were almost completely ineffective against the **Void Army**, plunging countless planets into panic. In response, the major powers united to form the **Galactic Alliance**.

Despite desperate struggle, the galaxy remained powerless against the Void Army’s advance. Many planets were consumed, leaving only a handful still habitable—**Ceres Major**, **Earth**, **Zhey Auna**, and **Blight**.

After four years of grueling resistance, the **Galactic Alliance Army** barely managed to withstand two waves of invasion.

**Year 4 of the Alliance**, Blight was struck by a massive assault. The Alliance Army paid a devastating price but ultimately repelled the enemy, securing the first victory in the Alliance’s history. The planet was left completely ruined and was renamed the **Blight Sector**, becoming the frontier of the Alliance. This battle became known as the **War of Blight**.

For a long time afterward, the galaxy did not face another large-scale invasion.

**Year 10 of the Alliance**, a galaxy-wide turning point occurred. During a routine military medical examination, a new genetically mutated race was discovered through **blood testing**—the **Shipsians**.

Because of their extraordinary combat abilities, they were incorporated into the military and trained as natural-born soldiers.

And from that point onward, the story of *Reburn the Galaxy* begins.

## Races

### Shipsians

A unique race that appears no different from ordinary humans, yet possesses extraordinary abilities.

They are unaffected by the vacuum of space and can operate freely without spacesuits. Within their bodies lies a hidden organ that, once activated, allows them to manipulate immense energy—enabling them to wield weapons beyond the capability of normal humans.

They are naturally resilient, physically superior, and possess terrifying mobility—making them perfect warriors.

All Shipsians are obligated to serve in the **Galactic Alliance Army**, but only the elite become **Shipsian warriors**, a special class specializing in solo combat.

Those who do not qualify serve in other roles within the human military.

Shipsian warriors possess powerful and unique **Main Weapons**, as well as support systems known as **Aura** and **Zen**, while non-elite Shipsians typically have access to only one of these.

This long-hidden race was discovered after the **War of Blight**. Unlike ordinary species, Shipsians do not inherit their traits through reproduction. Instead, they are the result of **postnatal genetic mutation** in humans.

For this reason, every child undergoes testing at the age of thirteen to identify hidden Shipsians among the population.

---

### Ana-Shipsians

A rare and exceptional group of Shipsians.

Those who commit crimes or refuse their duty to enlist are stripped of their right to use **Main Weapons**, **Aura**, and **Zen**. They are given the title **“Ana”**, which means “traitor” in the ancient language of Blight.

---

### Beastfolk

Natives of certain planets who possess animal-like traits.

Though they may appear fierce, they are among the most honest beings in the universe.

## Planets

### Ceres Major

The largest inhabited planet in the Galactic Alliance.

---

### Zhey Auna

The second-largest inhabited planet.

It once housed a major base of the **Galactic Alliance Army**, but after the **War of Blight**, the base was relocated to the **Prometheus Array**.

---

### Earth

A blue planet, third in population among inhabited worlds.

---

### Prometheus Array

A small artificial planet made entirely of metal.

It serves as the central command and technological core of the **Galactic Alliance Army**. Nearly all **Shipsian warriors** receive training here and live collectively.

---

### Gau Prime

A highly advanced technological world and the second-largest base of the Alliance Army.

It is primarily occupied by human military forces, with a large civilian population as well.

---

### Arcology

A small but resource-rich planet.

It is home to abundant natural ecosystems, serves as a natural laboratory for scientists, and functions as a vacation destination for the wealthy.

---

### Dovacoon Forge

A planet rich in mineral resources, known as the industrial center of the galaxy.

Nearly the entire planet is covered in heavy industry, and all three types of Shipsian equipment are forged here.

---

### Blight Sector

A desolate wasteland.

Once a prosperous world known as Blight, it was devastated during a massive war defending the galaxy. All life was extinguished, and its inhabitants evacuated, leaving behind only ruins and Alliance outposts.

Few humans can survive there—only a handful of stationed Shipsian warriors remain.

---

## Other

### Warp Gate

*Praise the Warp Gate!*

One of the greatest inventions in history.

Warp Gates allow instantaneous travel between linked points, revolutionizing interstellar transportation. They drastically reduce travel time for civilians and serve as a critical tool for warriors to rapidly deploy and withdraw from battle.
