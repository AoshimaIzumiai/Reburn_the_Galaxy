# Chapter 5: Her Will

> “No one knows when this war will end.
> All we can do… is pave the road toward that unknown future.”
> — Foenis, protagonist of *The Void: Anger of the Deity*


## 1

Phoenix and Disar saw it too.

With overwhelming force, the super battleship advanced—its shadow swallowing the faint starlight, leaving only the ominous red glow of its cannons.

Disar’s blue eyes were swallowed by darkness. Fear spread across her face.

“Phoenix… that’s…”

Her voice trembled.

“That’s… the Roc…”

Phoenix tightened her grip on the control lever without realizing it.

Voices came through the communicator.

“Neni, take out the turrets.”

“Copy.”

In the distance, the brown figure—Shogun—was still being chased by swarms of Sparrow and small-class enemies. Crimson-purple blade flashes cut through space, scattering debris along his path.

Meanwhile, Neni moved into open space.

She tossed her sniper rifle over her shoulder—it locked into a rail behind her. Instantly, the metallic device on her chest unfolded, extended, assembled—forming a launcher.

She raised her right arm, aiming with her fingers.

Light itself seemed to gather from the vacuum, condensing into a blazing blue core at her chest.

Two seconds.

The radar chimed twice—

A massive beam erupted.

The **Mega Laser** tore through space, striking the enemy warship in an instant.

The darkness of space shattered in blinding light. Phoenix and Disar raised their arms to shield their eyes.

No sound in vacuum—only alarms screaming:

**“High-energy discharge detected.”**

When the blue light finally faded, Phoenix saw it—

Two turrets destroyed.

“Guided turrets neutralized.”

Neni reported calmly.

“Take out the Doomsday. I’ll handle the escorts.”

“My weapon fires head-on.”

“Then take the Sparrows.”

“…Fine. I’ll take the Roc.”

Shogun accelerated sharply, breaking free from pursuit. He charged straight at the Roc.

At the moment of impact—

A burst of blade light.

Space itself seemed to warp.

The turret base shattered instantly.

“Rotary cannon destroyed.”

He veered upward, escaping the explosion perfectly.

Green flashes from Neni struck the hull and the **Doomsday** turret.

But her weapon required direct alignment.

A faint red glow began to coat her body.

“Neni. Three seconds.”

The turret finished charging.

She gritted her teeth—and was forced to retreat.


## 2

Phoenix thought she was seeing things.

The Doomsday turret—

**It wasn’t supposed to rotate.**

But it did.

The massive red beam turned—

And swept toward Neni.

Everything changed in an instant.

“Full speed—get out of there!”

Even Shogun sounded shaken.

He abandoned his target and rushed toward the turret’s blind spot.

Neni pushed her thrusters to the limit.

Three seconds.

Phoenix counted in her head.

Three.

Two—

“Stop already!”

But the beam didn’t stop.

Why?!

The battlefield fell into chaos.

“Hold on—it’ll burn out!”

But her own weapon trapped her—she couldn’t disengage.

“I can’t hit it! Damn Main Weapon—!”

“Shogun, destroy the Doomsday!”

“Copy—”

A flash of blue.

Static.

“Shogun?”

“Captain, respond!”

Silence.

Phoenix stared at the display:

**G.A.A.S. Shogun — OFFLINE**

Her heart dropped.

“What happened?!”

A broken voice returned.

“…systems… malfunction…”

Then gone again.

“System failure?! How?!”

“Phoenix—what’s that?!”

Disar pointed—

A small enemy ship.

Its turret glowing blue.

“…Stun? It’s a stun weapon!”

A new weapon.

Shogun must have been hit.

They had to warn Neni.

Phoenix slammed the comm button—

“Stun weapon—!!!”

Too late.

The blue shot struck.

Neni’s left thruster died instantly.

“Hit! Thruster failure!”

She struggled—but one thruster wasn’t enough.

The Doomsday beam closed in.

“…So this is it.”

She smiled bitterly.

“Damn Void Army…”

“Life and death… but a fleeting glance.”


## 3

Phoenix moved.

Without thinking.

She switched weapons.

“Four light machine guns online. 400 rounds.”

Fire.

BOOM—

Blue streams tore into the Roc.

“Phoenix, are you crazy?!”

Disar screamed.

Phoenix didn’t respond.

“100 rounds remaining.”

“PHOENIX!!”

Disar saw it—

That stun ship.

Aimed at them.

Phoenix’s golden eyes burned—

Only anger.

Only resolve.


## 4

—Someone was calling her.

—Disar.

But Phoenix couldn’t hear.

Only the reticle.

Only the target.

Only the Roc.

Gunfire filled her world.

“Machine gun connection lost.”

No hesitation.

Switch weapons.

Now.

“Heavy cannon connecting.”

“Warning: entering enemy range.”


## 5

“PHOENIX!!!”

Despair filled Disar’s voice.

Phoenix’s attack worked—

The Doomsday beam stopped—just before hitting Neni.

Then—

It turned.

Toward them.

“No… no…”

“Run!!”

Neni shouted.

But there was nowhere to go.

No track.

No shield.

No escape.

Time stretched.

“...We’re going to die…”

Disar collapsed.

Her eyes drifted—

To Phoenix.


## 6

Phoenix could hear everything.

The loading.

The gears.

The metal.

She saw everything.

The glowing cannon.

The burning red void.

She gripped the firing handles.

“WARNING.”

Red light flooded the cabin.

But her eyes burned brighter.

CLACK.

BOOM.

The shells fired.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Her arms screamed in pain—

But she kept pulling.

She became the machine.

“CLACK! CLACK! CLACK!”

The Roc’s hull exploded—

But the Doomsday still stood.

Three seconds.

How long had it been?

Time stretched endlessly.

Sweat soaked her face.

Her expression twisted—

Consumed by pure killing intent.

“Phoenix!”

Disar rushed forward—

And hugged her.

Three seconds.

The Doomsday fired.

Darkness fell.

A tidal wave of red light.

“AAAAAAAHHHHHHH—!!!”

Phoenix screamed—

Firing everything she had—

Charging straight into the light—

Her eyes filled with madness.

“AAAAAAAHHHH—!!!”

The beam swallowed everything.

Her voice—

Vanished.

Silence.


## 7

In the haze—

Someone looked up at the sky.

The stars—

Were not stars.

They were gun barrels.

“I’ll go.”

“Just me.”

“You must live.”

“Through this war… and every war…”

“Live to victory.”

“Live to the end.”

She turned.

“I will die here.”

“Carry my will.”

Tears—

That shouldn’t exist.

“Live on… my love.”

She smiled.

“I love you.”


## 8

Darkness.

Beep. Beep.

“She’s waking up.”
