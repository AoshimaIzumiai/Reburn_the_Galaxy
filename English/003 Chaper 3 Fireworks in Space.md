# Chapter 3: Fireworks in Space

## 1

Ceres Major Spaceport XI, Departure Hall.

It wasn’t any kind of holiday, yet the hall was still packed shoulder to shoulder. Ceres Major was not only the most populated planet—it was also a massive transportation hub. Its sheer size meant countless spaceports, constantly supplying resources from Ceres to the entire Galactic Alliance.

The overhead broadcast suddenly boomed like thunder, cutting through the noise—only for the chatter to surge back again moments later.

Phoenix sat alone on a metal bench, hugging two backpacks, watching the endless flow of people as she waited for her friend to return.

“Praise the Warp Gate!”

An advertisement flashed across the electronic screen beside her.

A civilian Warp Gate ad, she realized, glancing over. On the screen, several attractive models enthusiastically introduced the product.

“This is really not the place for it,” Phoenix muttered under her breath. “This is a traditional spaceship port—people here hate Warp Gates. Not even one in a thousand in this hall could afford it.”

“Warp Gates—the new tool for interstellar travel. Save your precious time! Officially supported by the Galactic Alliance Army, featuring the same configuration used by the military. Travel anytime, anywhere, whenever you want! For only 29,999 Galactic Credits…”

Yeah, that’s the problem.

Phoenix did the math in her head. Fast, sure—but that price was basically a month’s salary for an average Ceres worker… Ads like this should be shown on that rich-people planet called Arcology instead.

“Sorry to keep you waiting, Phoenix!”

Disar emerged from the crowd, holding two drinks. Phoenix smiled as she took one and handed the backpacks back to her.

The ad continued looping on the screen.

“Warp Gate ads really are everywhere,” Disar said, glancing at it too.

“I do kind of want to try it,” Phoenix admitted. “It used to be military-only. Civilian use is still pretty new…”

“When we get back from Zhey Auna, let’s try it?” Disar suggested with interest.

“But it’s expensive!”

“Did you forget? If we test positive as Shipsians, there’s a 28,000 credit subsidy… plus the scholarships from our last two top-five rankings…”

“Wait—doesn’t that mean it’s basically free?!”

“Shh! Keep your voice down…”

Phoenix had raised her voice without realizing it, and Disar quickly covered her mouth.

“Flight Z0716 to Zhey Auna is about to arrive. Passengers, please proceed to the platform.”

The announcement came right on time.

“It’s here, Phoenix.”

“Let’s go, Disar.”


## 2

The interior of a space vessel was completely different from an airplane. Instead of rows of seats, it was arranged more like sleeper train compartments—small private rooms.

Pushing through the crowd, Disar and Phoenix finally found theirs. A standard double cabin. The platform outside was visible through the window. A long table sat in the center, with convertible sofas on either side that could double as beds—essential for a journey lasting over ten hours.

They had just stowed their luggage beneath the table when the overhead speakers came on.

“Attention passengers. Welcome aboard space vessel Z0716. We will depart from Ceres Major and arrive at Zhey Auna after 19 Ceres hours and nine warp jumps. During warp, you may experience dizziness or discomfort. Please press the call button if you need assistance…”

Phoenix and Disar listened with interest.

“This vessel is a Dovacoon 194 model, equipped with safety systems. Escape pods are located at the rear. All routes are within Alliance-controlled safe zones. Gravity simulation will begin shortly. The vessel is about to depart. We wish you a pleasant journey.”

Phoenix curiously flipped through the travel handbook on the table, only to be interrupted as Disar handed her a stack of papers.

“I prepared a full guide for this trip,” Disar said proudly. “When we’ll pass Warp Gates, when we’ll see specific planets—I spent half a month gathering all this information!”

“Wow… that’s amazing, Disar…” Phoenix eagerly took it and flipped through. Every sight was marked down to the exact minute and second—even sleep time was carefully planned.

“Even though Zhey Auna and Ceres Major both have 24-hour days, their day-night cycles are very different… so adjusting your schedule is important.”

Disar pointed at the notes, clearly pleased with herself.

Phoenix weighed the stack in her hand. “So what’s our first stop?”

“Right here. Before entering space, the ship goes through a launch track. You’ll be able to see the entire Ceres Major from above. In about thirty minutes, we’ll take our first rest.”

Even ships used Warp Gate technology—just shared versions that were much cheaper than private ones.

The vessel began to move slowly. Phoenix rushed to the window, only to realize it wasn’t the ship moving—but the launch rail beneath it.

Soon, the vessel was rotated upright and began ascending along the track into a tunnel-like launch shaft.

Boom!

The sudden roar made both of them jump. Outside, the engines glowed blue. The scenery blurred rapidly as the ship shot upward through the shaft.

In the next instant, the tunnel vanished—replaced by darkness.

No—not complete darkness.

Tiny points of light revealed countless stars. Disar quickly turned back to take in the breathtaking view.

Ceres Major—the most populated planet in the galaxy. Now, the mother that nurtured countless lives finally revealed her true form.

“So beautiful… this is Ceres Major.”

Disar spoke from the heart.

“So magnificent…”

Phoenix was practically pressed against the window.

“Alright, that’s our first sightseeing stop done,” Disar said, turning back and tidying the bed. “Next is an hour and a half of rest. After that, we’ll see a Warp Gate jump.”

“Let me look a bit longer…” Phoenix protested. “I’m not done yet.”

“Fine—but you have to sleep within fifteen minutes, or you won’t adjust your schedule.”


## 3

“…come back… vanguard…”

Phoenix felt dazed.

This is a dream. My dream.

Someone was calling her.

Everything was hazy. Someone seemed very close, saying something…

“flash… dead… retreat…”

Dead? Who died?

She wanted to ask—but in the next moment, the voice drifted farther away.

“don’t… go…”

*Crack—*


## 4

A sharp noise pierced the air.

Half-asleep, Phoenix pulled the blanket over her head.

“Click… crack… zzzzt—”

“Hey… Disar… your alarm is so loud…”

“Hey—Disar—wake up—”

Phoenix sighed and sat up.

“Phoenix… stop… three more minutes…”

“Huh? Then what’s that noise?”

“What noise… you’re imagining things…”

Disar replied groggily.

“You don’t hear it? That crackling sound, like electricity?”

“No…”

“Really?”

“I said no!”

Disar sat up, rubbing her eyes.

“Fine… waking up two minutes early won’t kill me…”

Phoenix frowned toward the window—then suddenly relaxed.

“Disar, look—it’s a Warp Gate—”

Disar instantly woke up and looked outside.

A massive ring-shaped structure floated in space. Positioned along an asteroid’s orbit, the Warp Gate hummed as it detected the approaching vessel.

“First time seeing one… it’s incredible.”

“Yeah… how do you think it works—?!”

Before they could finish—

A section of the Warp Gate suddenly exploded into a burst of dazzling sparks.

“…Huh?”

The humming stopped. The faint blue glow vanished.

Something broke through the smoke.

Phoenix and Disar recognized the red-gray-white ship instantly.

It unleashed a barrage of red bullets and charged straight toward them.

“—The Void Army!!”

At the same moment they screamed, alarms blared throughout the ship.

“Warning. Enemy attack. Shields activated. Automatic defense systems engaged. Please remain calm. Escape pods are preparing for deployment.”

Disar grabbed her bag and flung open the cabin door. The corridor was already in chaos—alarms and screams drowning out the repeated “Please remain calm.”

“Phoenix, hurry!”

Phoenix grabbed her bag and was swept into the crowd.

She didn’t even need to walk—the crowd carried her forward.

Through the porthole, she saw terrified reflections of passengers. A pale purple shield enveloped the ship.

Her mind raced.

The Warp Gate was damaged—no immediate escape. Two possibilities remained: destroy the enemy with onboard defenses, or evacuate before the shield collapsed.

Dovacoon 194 defenses… top-mounted auto cannons… heavy machine guns below…

Red bullets slammed into the shield as the ship sped forward, trying to escape.

Ding—

A flash of purple light struck the shield.

Speed Laser!?

The ship shook violently. Panic surged.

That single beam was only the beginning.

Seven or eight beams from medium-sized enemy ships hammered the shield in succession.

Static crackled over the speakers.

“Captain, shield integrity has dropped to sixty percent!”

The internal channel had accidentally been broadcast.

“Damn Speed Lasers! What about the escape pods?!”

“Ready, sir! Decoys can be deployed anytime!”

“What about the Galactic Alliance Army?!”

“Communication lost after the last impact!”

“Then hurry—”

BOOM—

The shield tore open.

A red laser pierced through, destroying the top auto cannon.

Then came more purple beams—shredding the shield into nothingness.

The ship’s defenses were wiped out one by one. Engines died. Lights flickered.

“Proceed to escape pods immediately—”

“Shield integrity at ninety-eight percent!”

“Top cannons destroyed!”

“Communications offline!”

“Right engine down!”

“We’re doomed, Captain!”

“Deploy decoys. Prepare escape pods.”


## 5

Screams filled the ship.

Time stretched unbearably.

Phoenix saw the sign: 10 meters to escape pods.

“Phoenix! Did you hear that?!”

Disar’s voice echoed.

“Yes!”

“Use it—now!!”

The medal.

Aries had said—

“Press the button three times to call Alliance support.”

Phoenix reached for her pocket—but couldn’t move.

“Can’t get it—!”

She forced space open, grabbed it—

Finally.

She raised the medal overhead.

Boom—

The ship shook violently.

People were thrown into the air.

Phoenix barely held onto a railing.

In that brief moment, both of them were pushed into the escape pod entrance.

“Phoenix! Are you okay?!”

“I’m fine—but the medal—”

It slipped from her hand.

“Where did it go?!”

A flash of purple light illuminated the cabin.

“There!!” Disar shouted.

“Where?!”

“Outside the entrance!”

“I’ll get it!”

Phoenix forced her way back—but the crowd was too strong.

“Fifteen seconds until hatch closure.”

“Phoenix, you won’t make it back!!”

“Ten seconds.”

The medal was visible.

Five meters away.

“Gravity simulation shutting down.”

Phoenix broke free—

Ran—

“Five…”

“Four…”

“Three—”

Gravity vanished.

She slammed into the wall.

“Two—”

“Phoenix!!”

“One—”

In that single second, Disar made her choice.

She kicked off—

Flying straight toward the medal.

“Hatch closed.”

Both doors sealed.

The escape pod detached.

Disar grabbed the medal—

And slammed into the wall beside Phoenix.

They didn’t make it.

“Disar—press it—!”

One. Two. Three.

She pressed the button.

It lit up.

“This is the Galactic Alliance Army. Distress signal received. Please report your situation.”
