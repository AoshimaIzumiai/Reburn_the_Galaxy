# 📜 Introduction | 作品简介

《银河浴火》（Reburn the Galaxy）是基于 Firi Games 旗下游戏 *Phoenix II* 创作的正剧向同人作品。本文采用将游戏中的战机（Ships）进行拟人化的方式进行创作，以渴望成为 **希普斯战士（Shipsian Warrior）** 的少女 **凤凰（Phoenix）** 为主视角，讲述她与挚友 **戴沙（Disar）** 在银河对抗 **虚空军（Void Army）** 的战争中所经历的一系列成长与故事。


*Reburn the Galaxy* is a serious-tone fanfiction based on the game *Phoenix II* developed by Firi Games.The story anthropomorphizes the ships from the original game.
It follows **Phoenix**, a girl who dreams of becoming a **Shipsian Warrior**, and her close friend **Disar**, as they grow and struggle through the galactic war against the **Void Army**.
